Interrogating Privatization

This resource is for persons seeking information on how to critically analyze the privatization of a publicly-funded good or service. Each chapter covers tools or concepts which, in rough sequence, walk readers through a method for assessing the relative value of private versus public delivery. The chapters are free to download. Borrow one, read it, and leave a note in the margin for the next visitor.
